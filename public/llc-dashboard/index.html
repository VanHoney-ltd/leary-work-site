Privacy Comparison

Delaware

Good for business-law reputation, moderate privacy. Public-facing filings may be less owner-revealing than some states, but registered agent records, bank KYC, tax filings, contracts, and beneficial ownership rules can still identify control persons.

Wyoming

Strong privacy reputation and low owner-disclosure footprint. It is not magic anonymity; lenders, banks, IRS, payment processors, and registered agents can require real identity information.

New Mexico

Strong privacy and low annual filing friction. It is often the simplest privacy-friendly state for small remote companies, but Iowa registration reduces practical privacy if the business is visibly operated in Iowa.

Personal Credit Scenario: 550 Score

A 550 personal credit score affects bank approvals, business credit cards, SBA lending, personal guarantees, interest rates, vendor terms, and equipment/software financing. A new EIN does not erase personal underwriting, and EIN-only funding should not be treated as realistic startup capital.

Month 0-3: mostly self-funded, prepaid tools, secured card, business checking, basic vendor accounts, clean bookkeeping.
Month 3-6: small vendor terms may become possible if early payments are clean and records are organized.
Month 6-12: possible low-limit business credit, still likely with a personal guarantee.
Year 1-2: better odds if revenue, tax filings, bookkeeping, bank balances, and payment history are clean.

Trump / SBA Funding and Policy Context

These are official programs and policy references. They are not guaranteed grants, and most loans require underwriting, repayment ability, documentation, and often a personal guarantee.

  • SBA 7(a) Loan Program: primary SBA business loan program. Official source.
  • SBA 504 Loan Program: long-term fixed-asset financing, usually real estate/equipment. Official source.
  • SBA Microloans: smaller loans through intermediary lenders. Official source.
  • SBA Lender Match: tool to connect with SBA-approved lenders. Official source.
  • Guaranteeing Fair Banking for All Americans: executive order focused on fair banking access; use as banking-access policy context, not a grant. Official source.
  • One Big Beautiful Bill Act / small business tax deduction: tax-policy context, not direct startup funding. Official source.
  • SBA FY2025 capital access report: SBA reported major 7(a), 504, and SBIC capital access activity. Official source.

Entity Structure Options

Option A: Van Honey Ltd as parent

Van Honey Ltd as parent/holding company owns iON Data Management Systems and NovaSignal. iON operates software activity. NovaSignal operates marketing activity.

Use only after status verification. Old liabilities, delinquent reports, tax issues, or bank history can contaminate a clean launch.

Option B: New privacy LLC as parent

New Delaware, Wyoming, or New Mexico privacy LLC owns iON and NovaSignal. This can create a cleaner parent layer, but requires disciplined banking, contracts, bookkeeping, and intercompany records.

Option C: Clean new operating LLC

Keep Van Honey Ltd inactive until status is verified, then form one clean new operating LLC for initial revenue. Add separate DBAs, brands, or subsidiaries later when revenue justifies complexity.
